# SmartScholar 2026–2027 Priority Scholarship Catalogue

Research compiled 2026-08-09 from **official sources only** (government, agency,
university, and programme websites). No scholarship aggregator was used as an
authoritative source.

**Cycle definition:** "2026–2027" = the application round that opens in late
2026 / early 2027 for study intake beginning in 2027. Where a programme names
rounds by academic year (CSC, Stipendium Hungaricum), the intake-2027 round is
its "2027/2028" cycle.

**Honesty rule:** no 2026–2027 date has been inferred from previous years. A
deadline is reported for the 2026–2027 cycle **only** where an official source
published it. Otherwise the field reads exactly **"2026–2027 deadline not
officially published"** and the most recent officially published cycle is noted
clearly as a past cycle.

## Russia Priority

**Programme:** Quota of the Government of the Russian Federation (квота
Правительства РФ), administered by **Rossotrudnichestvo** via the state
information system "Education in Russia". Selection legal basis: Minobrnauki
Order No. 1378 of 3 Nov 2020. **Next intake = 2027/2028 academic year.**

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Country | Russia |
| Degree level | Bachelor / Specialist / Master / PhD, + 1-year free Russian preparatory course |
| Funding | Full tuition, monthly stipend (maintenance allowance), subsidised dorm (2,500–3,769 RUB/mo), prep-year tuition. **Not covered:** travel to/from Russia, health insurance, living costs |
| Eligible countries | Foreign citizens from 180+ countries; dual citizens (incl. Russian) only if permanently residing abroad and holding **no Russian-issued diploma**; not already enrolled in a Russian university |
| Fields | All fields offered by participating universities (~500+ universities, 741 for the previous cycle) |
| Requirements | Completed education appropriate to the level; competitive two-stage selection |

| 2026–2027 deadline | **Dec 15, 2026 (CIS countries)** / **Jan 15, 2027 (all other countries)** — per NUST MISIS official page; exact dates vary by country — confirm with the local Russian House/embassy. Results ~Apr–May 2027 |
