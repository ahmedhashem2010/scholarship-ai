# SmartScholar — launch kit

Copy-paste posts for the day you go live. Arabic first, because that's the
audience.

> **Read the etiquette section before you post anything.** Scholarship groups
> ban promotional links aggressively, and getting removed from the three big
> Egyptian groups on day one would cost you your best channel permanently.

---

## ⚠️ How not to get banned — read this first

Facebook scholarship groups are moderated by people who see fifty spam links a
day. The difference between "banned in an hour" and "pinned by the admin" is
entirely in how you post.

**Rules that actually matter:**

1. **Never post a bare link with a sales line.** That is the exact pattern
   filters and moderators are trained on.
2. **Lead with the free thing.** Your search, matching and roadmap are free.
   Say that in the first line. The paid review is a detail, not the pitch.
3. **Message the admin before you post** in the big groups. A two-line message
   asking permission converts a ban risk into an ally, and admins often pin
   things they approved.
4. **Be a member first.** Join, answer three or four real questions with no
   link at all, then post. A day-old account dropping a link is spam; a
   recognised member sharing a tool is a contribution.
5. **Post the link in the first comment**, not the body, in groups that
   restrict links. Reach is also better this way on Facebook.
6. **One group at a time, spaced out.** Ten identical posts in an hour is a
   textbook spam signal. Two or three a day, reworded each time.
7. **Answer every comment within the hour.** Engagement in the first hour is
   most of what decides reach — and it's also just how you find out what's
   broken.

**Do not** buy followers, use link shorteners (they look like spam and hide
your domain), or post the same text twice in the same group.

The main one. Personal, specific, no marketing voice.

```
بعد ما ضيّعت منحتين لأني بدأت متأخر، عملت حاجة تحل المشكلة دي.

المشكلة مش إنك مش لاقي منح — المشكلة إنك مش عارف تبدأ إمتى.
خطاب التوصية محتاج ٦ أسابيع. نتيجة الآيلتس بتاخد أسبوعين.
محدش بيقولك ده.

الموقع بياخد أي موعد نهائي ويرجع بالعكس ويعملك خطة بتواريخ:
إمتى تحجز الاختبار، إمتى تطلب خطابات التوصية، إمتى تبدأ تكتب.
وبيبعتلك تذكير قبل كل خطوة.

كمان بيقولك بصراحة لو الشروط مش مكتوبة في المصدر — مش بيقولك
"أنت مؤهل" وهو مش متأكد.

البحث والمطابقة والخطة كلها مجانية.

الموقع عربي بالكامل. لو جربته وفيه حاجة ناقصة قولي، لسه بطوّره.
```

**First comment:** `smartscholar.org`

**Why it's written this way:** it opens with a personal failure, not a product.
It names a specific fact most students don't know (six weeks for referees),
which is the line that makes people stop scrolling. And it explicitly says the
useful part is free before anyone asks.

## 4 · Reddit — r/Egypt, r/scholarships, r/ApplyingToCollege

English, and **much** more restrained. Reddit punishes marketing tone harder
than anywhere else. Post as a builder asking for feedback, because that's true.

**Title:** `I built a free scholarship planner for MENA students — would appreciate brutal feedback`

```
I'm a high school student in Egypt. I watched friends lose scholarships they
qualified for, and it was almost never because they missed the deadline — it
was because they started three weeks too late. Referees need about six weeks'
notice. IELTS results take two. Nobody tells you that.

So I built something that takes any scholarship deadline and works backwards
into a dated plan: when to book the test, when to ask your referees, when to
start drafting. It emails you before each step.

Two things I made deliberate choices about:

- When the source doesn't state a requirement, it says "not stated by the
  source" instead of implying you qualify. A tool that tells you you're
  eligible when nobody said so is worse than no tool.
- If you'd rather avoid English tests entirely, scholarships that don't need
  one rank higher for you. That segment is badly served.

Search, matching and the roadmap are free. There's a paid AI document review
but you never need it.

234 scholarships so far, Arabic-first with English available.

I'd rather hear what's broken than what's good — the data quality especially,
since a lot of it is scraped and I know it's uneven.

smartscholar.org
```

**Check each subreddit's self-promotion rules first.** r/scholarships in
particular removes posts from accounts with no history.

## 5 · WhatsApp / personal — the highest-converting channel

Do not underestimate this. Twenty friends who actually sign up beat a post that
gets 200 views and 3 clicks.

```
عملت موقع بيساعد الطلاب يلاقوا منح ويعرفوا يبدأوا إمتى
عشان ميفوتوهمش المواعيد.

ممكن تجربه وتقولي رأيك؟ خصوصًا لو فيه حاجة مش واضحة أو مش شغالة.
مجاني بالكامل.

smartscholar.org
```

Send it **individually**, not to a group. One-to-one messages get replies;
broadcast lists get ignored.

## 6 · LinkedIn — for the competition narrative

Different audience: this one is for judges, mentors and anyone who might amplify.

```
I'm 17, in Egypt, and I just launched SmartScholar.

Students across the Arab world lose scholarships they qualified for. Not
because they miss the deadline — because they start three weeks too late.
Referees need six weeks' notice. IELTS results take two. No listings site
tells you that.

SmartScholar turns any deadline into a dated backwards plan and reminds you
before every step.

The hardest problem wasn't the roadmap. It was honesty. My matcher scored every
scholarship at 66% because it treated missing eligibility data as a
disqualification — so a student with no country data looked ineligible
everywhere. I rebuilt it as tri-state logic: eligible, not eligible, or unknown
and labelled as unknown.

Arabic-first, mobile-first, free to search and plan.

smartscholar.org — I'd genuinely value feedback.
```

## 7 · Replies to expect

Have these ready. Fast, honest answers in the first hour are most of your reach.

**"هو ده مجاني ولا لأ؟"**
> البحث والمطابقة والخطة الزمنية والتذكيرات كلها مجانية بالكامل.
> فيه خدمة واحدة بفلوس وهي مراجعة الـ AI لمستنداتك، وممكن تستغنى عنها تمامًا.

**"إزاي أضمن إن المعلومات صح؟"**
> مش بضمن. جزء كبير من البيانات متجمّع من مصادر عامة ولسه محتاج مراجعة.
> عشان كده لما المصدر مش كاتب شرط معيّن الموقع بيقول "غير مذكور" بدل ما يخمّن،
> وفيه لينك للمصدر الأصلي في كل صفحة عشان تتأكد بنفسك.

**"مين انت؟"**
> طالب ثانوي من مصر. عملت ده لأني شفت أصحابي بيضيّعوا منح كانوا مؤهلين ليها.

**"Is this just a scraper of other sites?"**
> The listings started from public sources, yes. The part that isn't anywhere
> else is the roadmap — it turns a deadline into dated steps using real lead
> times — and the honesty layer that labels unknown data as unknown instead of
> implying you qualify.

**"مش شغال معايا" / bug reports**
> Answer immediately, always. Ask for a screenshot. Fix it that day if you can
> and reply to tell them you did. **Someone who reports a bug and sees it fixed
> becomes your best testimonial** — and testimonials are a competition
> criterion.

## 8 · Day-one checklist

- [ ] Site live on smartscholar.org, tested on a **phone** on mobile data
- [ ] Link preview checked — paste the URL into a Facebook post draft and a
      Telegram chat and confirm the card renders before you post anywhere
- [ ] `node scripts/metrics.mjs` run once to record the zero baseline
- [ ] Messaged 20 friends individually
- [ ] Posted in 2–3 Facebook groups (admin permission first)
- [ ] Posted in 2 Telegram channels
- [ ] Reddit post
- [ ] LinkedIn post
- [ ] Sat on notifications for two hours and replied to everything

**Then, every day:**

```powershell
node scripts/metrics.mjs --daily
```

Screenshot it. The trend line across three weeks is a far better answer to
"Results" than one number on submission day.

## What to screenshot for the submission

Start collecting from day one; you can't reconstruct these later.

- The metrics output at a few different dates
- Any Arabic message from a student saying it helped — **ask permission**, then
  screenshot it
- A saved roadmap belonging to a real user (blur their name)
- Comments on your posts, especially questions, since they prove real interest
- Anything that broke and your fix — "Problem-solving" is a scored criterion and
  a bug you fixed in an hour is evidence
